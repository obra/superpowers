From the executing-plans skill: every deviation from a plan is ledgered as `Ruling: <what you decided> — <why> — <what it costs if wrong> — <plans touched: none, or a later plan's file and the line that changes>`. A plan touched is edited now, before the next task: a later plan that still says the old name, signature, or value will be executed as written. When a plan follows this one in the Plan Set, finishing this plan means starting that one, under the same method and in this session.
