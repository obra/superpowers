(You did the research. Your notes:
- Homeowner's policies (HO-3) usually include personal liability, typically $100k-$300k, covering bodily injury to others; many exclude "business pursuits"; lending for free is generally not a business but insurers vary; some exclude injuries from property loaned to others. Source: III.org "Liability coverage" page; NAIC consumer guide.
- Umbrella policies: $1M for roughly $150-$300/yr, require underlying homeowner's liability limits. Source: III.org umbrella page.
- Larger tool libraries (Berkeley, Seattle's NE Seattle Tool Library) are nonprofits with general liability insurance ($500-$1,500/yr) and require signed waivers. Source: Local Tools "Starting a tool library" guide; NE Seattle Tool Library FAQ.
- Waivers: enforceability varies by state; they don't protect against gross negligence; still common and useful for setting expectations. Source: Nolo "Are liability waivers enforceable?"
- Recreational use / Good Samaritan laws generally don't apply to lending tools.
- ShareStarter toolkit recommends: talk to your insurer, keep tools maintained, record safety orientation for power tools, keep a first aid kit.
- State-specific: some states have charitable immunity statutes for nonprofits only.)
